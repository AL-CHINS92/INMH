        The honest bit

        No jokes for a minute.

        Jen,

        Most days I work hard to look like I’ve got it together. I did that for years before I knew why things felt so hard.

        So by evening, my tank is often empty. If I seem flat, I’m not checking out. I’m running on reserve.

        Some days I’m buzzing with energy out of nowhere. Then I’m moody for no reason I can name. Neither one is about you.

        I used to think I was lazy and careless. Turns out, I’m just wired differently.

        It doesn’t always look like it, but this isn’t me “trying” my best. It’s me doing my best.

        You understanding this means more than you know.
